*CHARTING RESEARCH HORIZONS*

What actually goes into modern AI research beyond training larger transformers?

This Monday, AISOC members are sharing a high-level overview of the research problems they’ve been tackling. Whether you want to get into academic research or just grasp the core intuitions behind these domains without wading through dense papers first, come see what our members are investigating :]

<div><strong>[ WHAT WE&#39;LL EXPLORE ]</strong></div>

**World Models:** How agents build internal representations to simulate and plan in complex environments.  
**Bayesian Theory of Mind (BToM):** Formalising how systems infer the hidden beliefs and intentions of other agents.  
**AI Safety:** Technical fundamentals of alignment, robustness, and reliable model behaviour.  
**Machine Unlearning:** Selectively removing data or capabilities from trained weights without retraining.
